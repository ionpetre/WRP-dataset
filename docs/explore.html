2021–2025, worry + harm: seven clusters agree closely. Four form a stronger recurring core, covering 69.90% of the 2025 reference weight. Traffic, crime and weather divisions vary more under refitting.

2021–2025, worry only: the selected three-cluster point partitions are identical on all 243 answer patterns. Between 92.12% and 98.00% of cross-refit pairs are also identical.

Including 2019: we did not establish a comparably robust shared partition. Its independent clustering remains a separate comparison.
