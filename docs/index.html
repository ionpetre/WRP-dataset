World Risk Poll

Patterns of worry recur around the world

We grouped people with similar answers in each global survey. Some combinations kept coming back. Concern about crime and water often appeared together. Another recurring pattern centred on traffic, crime and severe weather.

Explore the patterns

The patterns can recur even as their frequency changes

Because we can recognise the same patterns, we can see which become more or less common. Across the same 92 countries, the group marked by worry about traffic, crime and severe weather became more common from 2021 to 2025, while the mental-health worry group became less common.

The World Risk Poll, created by Lloyd’s Register Foundation and conducted by Gallup, asks people around the world about specific concerns and whether they or someone they know has experienced serious harm.
