What do people worry about together?

Two people can be equally worried about very different things. The World Risk Poll, created by Lloyd’s Register Foundation and conducted by Gallup, asks people around the world about specific concerns and whether they or someone they know has experienced serious harm.

When the same combinations recur, we can follow how common they are over time and ask whether people from different backgrounds share them.
