The website presents recurring combinations of worry and reported harm across World Risk Poll waves. The five-cluster evidence notebook contains editable checks and saved results for the shared clustering.

The main findings

The main worry–harm solution has five clusters across 2021, 2023 and 2025. Each wave was fitted independently with the same encoding, including all six worries and fourteen harm indicators. The lowest cross-wave 10th-percentile ARI is 0.904 and every cluster’s weighted Jaccard is at least 0.843 by the same criterion.

Independent fitting and later joint selection

Use the same moderately worried value q = 1/3 and worry weight α = 0.625. Fit seven weighted K-means centres independently in each wave. Match the centres to the 2025 cluster labels using the same 2025 reference respondents. Map the traffic, crime and severe-weather centres to one cluster and keep the other four separate. Each complete respondent receives the group of the nearest centre.

The comparison considered three through seven flat clusters with and without an indicator for very high crime and weather worry, as well as two fixed combinations of the seven centres. The five-cluster combination passed the original size and within-wave filters and the 0.80 cross-wave lower-decile targets. The older independent setting catalogue is retained.

Eligibility, coding and weights

Keep complete substantive answers for the shared worry–harm analysis, without imputation. Source worry codes very/somewhat/not become 2/1/0. For harm, personal only gives (1,0), known-person only (0,1), both (1,1), and neither (0,0). Unknown, refused and missing answers remain missing. The two indicators concern different recipients.

Code worry as 0/q/1, subtract the person’s mean and retain that mean as a reduced-influence coordinate. Full models scale these seven worry coordinates by √(α/6) and all fourteen harm indicators by √((1−α)/14). Five-worry models use their centred values and mean divided by √5. Weighted K-means minimizes weighted squared Euclidean distance. Every country has equal total fitting/reporting mass, retaining relative WGT within eligible country populations. These are not world-population shares.

Comparing definitions and frequencies

Definition comparisons apply both models to identical reference answers: 90,395 complete 2025 respondents in 92 common development countries. Ordinary ARI uses respondent counts and is not percentage accuracy. Weighted agreement uses optimal one-to-one matching. Each overlap-table row uses country/survey weights and sums to 100% before rounding.

The repeated-fit view uses fifty respondent and fifty country refits in each native development frame. Country subsampling retains approximately 80% of countries, stratified by region and income. Each refit assigns the same reference respondents. Fifty runs give 1,225 distinct within-wave pairs and 2,500 cross-wave pairs. The displayed ranges are p10–p90 and the dot is the median. They are conditional dependent distributions, not confidence intervals.

Frequency comparisons apply the same fitted 2025 model and its five-cluster map to each wave’s own respondents in the common country set. Differences are percentage points. Independent cross-sections do not identify individual transitions.

Rule descriptions

Search all 1,491,084 formulas in the full catalogue of up to three conditions. Keep rules with at least 80% precision on each wave’s native development population. Among those within one percentage point of the highest 2025 coverage, select the lowest cost, then higher 2025 precision and coverage, fewer questions and canonical formula order. The same selected rules also pass in the 92 common countries.

Cost is the number of condition occurrences in the search. “Few very high worries” means food is the only domain that may be rated very worried. Its searched form is V = 0 OR (food very worried AND V ≤ 1), so it has cost three. V counts very-worried answers across six worries. K counts the seven domains with harm to someone known. The exact-four wording in the harm rule is equivalent to the searched threshold because its other branch already covers K ≥ 5.

Country maps and distances

Country views include every country and area surveyed in the selected wave. The wave’s independently fitted model is applied unchanged to all respondents with complete answers, including countries not used for fitting. Grey is reserved for places absent from that survey wave. They compare national cluster mixtures by Hellinger distance, or original question marginals by total variation. Full original-answer distance uses the selected model’s allocation α. Each colour denominator is the median over distinct development-country pairs under the fixed 2025 model of the same input family. It stays fixed when another wave is shown.

The cluster-similarity overview projects square-root shares into two principal coordinates. Exact nearest-country ranks use the full distance. Colour axes are specific to a model, and country coverage can differ across waves. Income-group summaries are descriptive associations among reused country pairs. These maps describe the survey answers under fixed cluster definitions; displaying additional countries is separate from fitting or validating the clustering.

Separate worry-only analysis

The 2019–2025 analysis uses five common worry questions. Its four-cluster solution splits the crime-and-water cluster according to mental-health worry and meets the original 40% size cap. On the same respondents, agreement with the worry–harm clusters is low. Verification notebook.

Matched tests with and without harm

Fit weighted K-means independently in each wave using four or five clusters and either six worry questions or the five common questions without traffic. Both fits in each pair use the same complete development respondents and 0, 1/3 and 1 coding. The worry coordinates are held fixed. The second fit appends fourteen harm indicators at the main analysis weight. We compare the paired fits on each wave’s respondents in the 92 common development countries and check 50 matched respondent draws and 50 matched country draws. See the matched ARIs.

For the two main analyses, measure the change in the entire four-by-five membership table on the same 2025 respondents. Total variation is half the sum of the absolute changes in weighted cell shares. The harm composition chart uses each wave’s own matched respondents and shows the worry-only label among members of the harm cluster.
