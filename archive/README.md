# Historical and supplementary work

The current paper companion is [the complete clustering notebook](../paper/wrp_cross_wave_clustering.ipynb). Current website entry points are in `../docs/`.

`historical_benchmarks/docs/` preserves the previous website exactly as it stood before the final-model update. It includes the original 2023 T5 and frozen 2025 six-cluster views. It is outside the GitHub Pages publication root, absent from current navigation, and should not be used to identify the paper’s principal cross-wave models. Historical links and assets are retained for provenance and may depend on their original layout.

Older prediction experiments are supplementary research, not a dependency of the clustering companion. The existing public [2023 membership-prediction notebook](../weekly_notebooks/week06_predicting_frozen_risk_profile_membership.ipynb) is archived in place with its original support files and paths, preserving recorded hashes and worker compatibility. It is excluded from the main website narrative. The [original validation notebook](../weekly_notebooks/week05_validate_frozen_profiles.ipynb) is likewise historical supporting work.

The wider project's country-aware, incremental-outcome, stated-risk and cross-domain prediction studies retain their original research archive outside this clean clustering companion. This handoff does not repackage those studies as new public prediction implementations. EBM, CatBoost, TabM and stacking belong to older prediction comparisons, not to the dependencies of the current clustering notebook.

The original WARM-5/stored T5 and WRP2025_K6 assignments, definitions and result snapshots are preserved. Their held-out evaluations concern those models alone. A historical filename containing “profile” is preserved as a source identifier; revised scientific prose calls respondent groups clusters.
