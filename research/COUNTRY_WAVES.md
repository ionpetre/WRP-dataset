# Country comparisons

The current website uses the final seven-cluster full models and final three-cluster later-wave worry models. 2019 remains a separate within-wave alternative. Country maps use native development countries only. They compare Hellinger distance on cluster shares with weighted total variation on original responses. No old bootstrap or permutation result is attributed to the new models.

See [the complete companion](../paper/README.md), [current overview](../docs/index.html), [methods](../docs/methods.html) and [archive guide](../archive/README.md). The exact model definitions, fitting scope, populations, equations and aggregate inputs are documented together in the companion.
