# Country comparisons

The current website uses the final seven-cluster full models and final three-cluster later-wave worry models. 2019 remains a separate within-wave alternative. Country maps include every surveyed country and area, using each wave’s already fitted model unchanged. Grey means absent from the selected survey wave. Development-only clustering evidence remains separate; the original aggregates are preserved and numerically unchanged. They compare Hellinger distance on cluster shares with weighted total variation on original responses. No old bootstrap or permutation result is attributed to the new models.

See [the complete companion](../paper/README.md), [current overview](../docs/index.html), [methods](../docs/methods.html) and [archive guide](../archive/README.md). The exact model definitions, fitting scope, populations, equations and aggregate inputs are documented together in the companion.
